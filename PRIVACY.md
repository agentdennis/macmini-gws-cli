# Privacy policy: macmini-gws-cli

Last updated: 2026-10-22

- **Who uses it:** only its owner, on their own computer. There are no other users.
- **What it accesses:** read-only access to the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar.
- **What it does with it:** builds short summaries that stay on the owner's
  computer and are sent only to the owner.
- **Sharing:** Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, or
  used for advertising or to train AI models.
- **Storage and deletion:** data is kept only on the owner's computer. Access can
  be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- **Contact:** open an issue on this repository.
